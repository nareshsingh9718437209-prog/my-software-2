HOW TO OPEN THE WEBSITE
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html - it opens in your browser.

WHERE THINGS ARE
index.html   -> home page content (text, links)
css/style.css -> colours, fonts, layout, animations
js/main.js    -> slider, menus, scroll effects
images/       -> your pictures
pages/        -> other pages

TO CHANGE COLOURS: open css/style.css and edit the first block (":root").
Text in [square brackets] in index.html is placeholder text to replace.
