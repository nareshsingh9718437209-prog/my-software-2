Priority Support

Support via email and phone during business hours for active plans.

What priority support offers

Active plan customers can get assistance to resolve issues and keep business operations running smoothly.

  • Email support: raise queries and get help for product, setup, and usage issues
  • Phone support: speak with our support team during business hours for faster assistance
  • Guided help: support for common issues related to billing, GST, inventory, banking, reports, and more

When to use it

  • Issues affecting day-to-day operations or important workflows
  • Questions about features, configuration, or usage
  • Help understanding errors or getting back on track quickly

Availability

Priority support is available for active plans during business hours. Response times and coverage may vary by plan and issue type. For assistance, reach us through our support channels.
