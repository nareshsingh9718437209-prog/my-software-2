Privacy Policy

How DotERP collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: 2026

1. Overview

DotERP Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("DotERP", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use our website, products, and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly and information collected automatically:

  • Contact & account information: name, email address, phone number, company details, and login-related information when you contact us, request a demo/trial, create an account, or purchase services.
  • Business & transaction information: details needed to process orders, subscriptions, licenses, support requests, and billing.
  • Usage & technical data: device/browser information, IP address, pages visited, and interactions with our website to improve performance and security.
  • Communications: messages, feedback, and support requests you send to us.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our products and services.
  • To process purchases, subscriptions, licenses, and customer support.
  • To communicate with you about updates, offers, security notices, and service-related information.
  • To personalize and secure your experience, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers (such as hosting, payment, communication, or support providers) who process data on our behalf, and when required by law or to protect our rights, users, and services.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information as long as necessary to provide services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable laws. You can also opt out of non-essential communications. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to function properly, remember preferences, and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@doterp.example or visit our Contact page.
