Why businesses choose DotERP

A practical balance of simplicity, compliance, and complete business features.

  • One ecosystem: DotERP for complete operations, UdyamApp for simple billing, Q2Dot for bulk imports.

Which one should you pick?

Quick recommendation based on common business needs.

  • Small shop / billing-first: choose UdyamApp for quick GST invoices and simple accounting.
  • Growing SME / all-in-one: choose DotERP for accounting, inventory, GST, payroll, and reports.

  • Not sure yet: talk to our team for a requirement-based comparison and demo guidance.
