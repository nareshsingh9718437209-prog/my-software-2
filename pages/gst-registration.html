GST Registration Online in India: Process, Eligibility, Documents & GSTIN Guide

DotERP Solutions  |  Updated on Jun 22, 2026
30-second summary: GST Registration is the process by which a business gets a unique 15-digit Goods and Services Tax Registration Number (GSTIN) from the GST portal. Mandatory registration thresholds are ₹40 lakh for goods (₹20 lakh for special-category states) and ₹20 lakh for services. The entire process is online via the GST portal and typically takes 3-7 working days after document verification.

What is GST Registration?

GST Registration is the process by which a business taxpayer gets registered under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) law. Upon successful registration, the business receives a unique 15-digit identification number called the Goods and Services Tax Registration Number (GSTIN), issued by the GST portal. (updated on 11 June 2026)

Once registered, a business becomes legally authorised to collect GST from customers, claim input tax credit (ITC) on purchases, and file GST returns. GST registration transforms an unorganised business into a recognised taxpayer in the formal Indian economy.

Importance of GST Registration for Businesses

GST registration brings businesses into the formal tax system. It allows them to seamlessly integrate with the national market, claim input tax credit, and expand to interstate trade without tax barriers. It is also a prerequisite for participating in government tenders, e-commerce platforms and B2B supply chains.

Eligibility for GST Registration

Not every business needs to register for GST. Eligibility depends on the type of business, its turnover, and the nature of its supplies. The GST law specifies clear thresholds and special cases that trigger mandatory registration.

Turnover Limits for Mandatory Registration

Businesses supplying goods must register for GST if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs in a financial year. For special category states (such as Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Puducherry, Sikkim, Telangana, Tripura and Uttarakhand) the threshold is ₹20 lakhs. For service providers, the threshold is ₹20 lakhs across India (₹10 lakhs in special category states).

Special Cases Requiring Registration

Certain businesses must register irrespective of turnover, including: inter-state suppliers of goods and services, e-commerce operators and sellers operating through e-commerce platforms, input service distributors, non-resident taxable persons, persons deducting TDS under GST, and agents supplying on behalf of others.

Voluntary Registration Benefits

Even if a business is below the threshold, it can register voluntarily. This allows the business to claim input tax credit (ITC) on its purchases, build credibility with larger B2B customers, and present itself as a compliant supplier.

Benefits of GST Registration

GST registration offers several tangible benefits that go beyond tax compliance.

  • Tax savings â Registered businesses can claim input tax credit (ITC) on the GST paid for business purchases, which reduces the overall tax burden and prevents cascading of taxes.
  • Legal and operational benefits â Registration makes the business a legally recognised entity that can trade interstate without restrictions, and allows seamless movement of goods across state borders.
  • Business growth â A GSTIN adds credibility. Many B2B customers and large procurement contracts insist on working only with GST-registered suppliers. Registered businesses can issue tax invoices with consecutive serial numbers that comply with GST rules.

Documents Required for GST Registration

The documents required for GST registration vary based on the type of business entity. Below is the list of documents needed for the most common entity types.

For Individuals

  • Aadhaar card
  • PAN card
  • Passport-sized photograph
  • Address proof (passport, driving license, utility bills like electricity or water bill)

For Companies

  • Certificate of incorporation
  • PAN card of the company
  • Address proof of the company's place of business
  • Bank account statements

For Partnerships

  • Partnership deed
  • PAN card of the partnership firm
  • Aadhaar card of partners
  • Address proof of the principal place of business

Make sure all documents are clear, legible and in PDF or JPEG format before uploading. The name and address on all documents must match exactly with the application details.

How to Apply for GST Registration Online (Step-by-Step)

The entire GST registration process is online through the official GST portal. Follow these steps:

  1. Step 1: Visit the GST Portal â Go to www.gst.gov.in and click on "Services".
  2. Step 2: New Registration â Under the "Services" tab, click on "Registration" and then select "New Registration".

How to Track Your GST Application Status Using ARN

Once you submit your GST registration application, you receive an Application Reference Number (ARN). This 15-digit number lets you track the status of your application until the GSTIN is issued.

Step-by-Step Process to Check GST ARN Status

  • Visit the GST Portal at www.gst.gov.in
  • Navigate to the Application Status section
  • Click on: Services → Registration → Track Application Status
  • Enter ARN details
  • Select the ARN option
  • Enter your ARN number
  • Complete the CAPTCHA
  • Click on Check Status
  • Click Search to view the result

What ARN Status Indicates

  • Pending for Processing â Application is under review by the tax officer.
  • Pending for Clarification â Additional details or documents are required from the applicant.
  • Approved â GSTIN will be issued shortly and a registration certificate will be generated.
  • Rejected â Application not accepted. Reapply after correcting the cited issues.

GST Registration Verification & Approval

After submission, the application goes through verification by GST authorities before the GSTIN is issued.

Verification Process by GST Authorities

  1. Document Scrutiny â Officers verify the authenticity and consistency of all submitted documents.
  2. Physical Verification (if required) â In some cases officers may conduct a site visit to the principal place of business.
  3. Background Check â Cross-verification with PAN, Aadhaar and other government databases.

Common Reasons for Application Rejection and How to Avoid Them

  • Incomplete Information â Make sure every mandatory field is filled.
  • Discrepancies in Documents â Names and addresses across documents must match.
  • Incorrect Details â Re-check PAN, mobile number and email address.
  • Non-compliance Issues â Ensure all uploads meet the portal's format and size requirements.

Tips for a Successful Verification Process

  • Maintain Communication â Respond promptly to any clarification notice.
  • Stay Informed â Regularly check ARN status and emails from GST.
  • Follow-up if Required â Contact the jurisdictional officer if processing is unduly delayed.

Post-GST Registration Compliance

Once registered, the business must comply with ongoing GST obligations to remain in good standing.

Filing GST Returns and Maintaining Compliance

  • Frequency â File GSTR-1 (outward supplies) monthly or quarterly, and GSTR-3B (summary return) monthly.
  • Process â Reconcile sales and purchase data, then file returns before the due date.
  • Importance â Timely filing is mandatory to claim ITC and avoid penalties.

Important Deadlines and Penalties for Late Filing

  • Deadlines â GSTR-1 typically due by the 11th, GSTR-3B by the 20th of the following month.
  • Penalties â Late fee of ₹50 per day (₹20 for nil return), capped as per GST rules. Interest at 18% p.a. on delayed tax payment.

GST Registration Cancellation

A registered business can choose to cancel its GST registration when it no longer requires it. Cancellation can also be initiated by the proper officer in case of non-compliance.

Scenarios Where Cancellation is Applicable

  • Business Closure â The business has discontinued operations.
  • Transfer of Business Ownership â Business is merged, demerged or sold.
  • No Longer Eligible â Turnover has dropped below the threshold and registration is no longer required.

Step-by-Step Guide to Cancel GST Registration

  1. Access GST Portal â Log in to www.gst.gov.in using your credentials.
  2. Navigate to Services â Click on Services → Registration → Application for Cancellation of Registration.

FAQs

Is GST registration mandatory? +
Yes, GST registration is mandatory for businesses whose aggregate turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs for goods (₹20 lakhs in special category states) or ₹20 lakhs for services. Certain businesses like e-commerce operators and inter-state suppliers must register regardless of turnover.
What documents are required for GST registration? +
The required documents include PAN, Aadhaar, business address proof, bank statement, passport-sized photograph and entity-specific documents like certificate of incorporation (for companies) or partnership deed (for partnerships).
How long does it take to complete the GST registration process? +
After submitting the application and all documents, it typically takes 3 to 7 working days for the GST department to process and approve the application. The GSTIN is issued once verification is complete.
Can I cancel my GST registration? If yes, how? +
Yes. Log in to the GST portal, go to Services → Registration → Application for Cancellation of Registration, provide the reason and date, then submit using EVC or DSC.
What happens if I do not register for GST when I am required to? +
The department may levy a penalty of 10% of the tax due (minimum ₹10,000) for unintentional non-registration, or up to 100% of the tax due for deliberate evasion.
Do I need to apply for GST registration if I only make online sales? +
Yes. Suppliers selling through e-commerce platforms and inter-state online sellers must register for GST irrespective of turnover.
Can a foreign company register for GST in India? +
Yes. A foreign company making taxable supplies in India must register as a Non-Resident Taxable Person (NRTP) or Casual Taxable Person, depending on the nature and duration of its operations.
What is the process for GST registration if I have multiple business locations? +
You can register multiple places of business under a single GSTIN by adding them as additional places of business in the application. Separate GSTIN is required for each state where you operate.
What should I do if I exceed the GST registration threshold limit during the year? +
You must apply for GST registration within 30 days from the date you cross the threshold. The registration becomes effective from the date you became liable, not from the date of application.
