Different Tax Components under GST

Understand how each component flows between Central and State governments.

CGST (Central Goods and Services Tax) +
Collected by the Central Government on intra-state supplies. The centre gets half of the GST paid on transactions within a state.
SGST (State Goods and Services Tax) +
Collected by the State Government on intra-state supplies. The state gets the other half of the GST paid on within-state transactions.
UTGST (Union Territory Goods and Services Tax) +
Similar to SGST but applicable in Union Territories without legislature, collected by the UT administration.
IGST (Integrated Goods and Services Tax) +
Collected by the Central Government on inter-state supplies and imports. The centre later settles the appropriate share with the destination state.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is a GST Calculator? +
A GST Calculator is a free online tool that helps you compute GST on a given amount. It shows the GST breakup into CGST, SGST and IGST based on whether the supply is intra-state or inter-state, and works for both GST-inclusive and GST-exclusive prices.
What is the formula to calculate GST manually? +

What is the difference between GST Inclusive and GST Exclusive? +
GST Exclusive means GST is added on top of the listed price. GST Inclusive means the listed price already contains GST, so you need to back-calculate the taxable value and the GST portion.
How much is 18% GST on ₹1,000? +
18% GST on ₹1,000 is ₹180. So the final amount payable (GST-exclusive) is ₹1,180. If ₹1,000 is GST-inclusive, the taxable value is ₹847.46 and GST is ₹152.54.
What are CGST and SGST? +
CGST is the Central GST collected by the Central Government, and SGST is the State GST collected by the State Government. Both apply on intra-state supplies and together equal the full GST rate.
What is IGST? +
IGST is Integrated GST, collected by the Central Government on inter-state supplies and imports. The full GST rate is charged as IGST when goods or services move from one state to another.

Why should I use an online GST calculator? +
It eliminates manual calculation errors, saves time when preparing invoices or returns, and helps you present a clear tax breakup to customers and auditors.
