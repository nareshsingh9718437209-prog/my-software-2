GSTR-3B: Summary Return Filing & Tax Payment Guide

DotERP Solutions  |  Updated on 2026
30-second summary: GSTR-3B is a summary GST return where taxpayers declare outward supplies, eligible ITC, and tax liability, and make tax payments for the period. Accurate GSTR-3B filing helps ensure correct tax payment and ITC claims.

On this page

  • What is GSTR-3B?
  • Who Should File GSTR-3B?
  • Details Declared in GSTR-3B
  • How to File GSTR-3B
  • Tax Payment & ITC Offset
  • Tips for Accurate Filing
  • FAQs

What is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is a self-declared summary return for reporting taxable outward supplies, inward supplies liable to reverse charge (if any), eligible input tax credit (ITC), and tax payable for a tax period. It is also used for discharging GST liability.

Who Should File GSTR-3B?

Regular GST-registered taxpayers generally file GSTR-3B for each tax period (monthly or as applicable under QRMP/payment schedules). Nil filing may be required when there are no transactions, as per applicability.

Details Declared in GSTR-3B

  • Outward supplies: Taxable, exempt, nil-rated and non-GST supplies summary.
  • Reverse charge supplies: Inward supplies liable to reverse charge (if any).
  • Eligible ITC: ITC available/claimed/reversed as applicable.
  • Tax liability: IGST, CGST, SGST/UTGST and cess payable.
  • Interest/late fee: If applicable for the period.

How to File GSTR-3B

  1. Reconcile data: Match sales, purchases, and ITC with books and GSTR-1/GSTR-2B where relevant.
  2. Prepare summary values: Compute outward tax, eligible ITC, and net liability.
  3. Enter details on portal: Fill GSTR-3B tables/sections for the period.
  4. Offset liability: Use eligible ITC and/or cash balance as per rules.
  5. Pay and file: Make payment (if required) and file using DSC/EVC.

Tax Payment & ITC Offset

GST liability in GSTR-3B can be paid using available ITC (subject to set-off rules) and cash ledger balance. Ensure ITC claimed is eligible and supported by records to avoid reversals, interest, or notices later.

Tips for Accurate Filing

  • Reconcile GSTR-1, books, and ITC data before filing GSTR-3B.
  • Claim only eligible ITC and reverse ineligible credit where required.
  • Verify tax totals and payment challans before submission.
  • File on time to reduce late fees/interest risk.

FAQs

What is GSTR-3B? +
GSTR-3B is a summary GST return for declaring outward supplies, ITC, and tax liability, and for making tax payment.
Is GSTR-3B the same as GSTR-1? +
No. GSTR-1 reports outward supply details (invoice-level/summary details), while GSTR-3B is a summary return for tax liability and payment.
Can I claim ITC in GSTR-3B? +
Yes, eligible ITC can be claimed in GSTR-3B, subject to GST rules and reconciliation/supporting records.
Do I need to pay tax before filing GSTR-3B? +
If there is net tax liability after ITC offset, payment must be made (as applicable) before filing the return.
What if I make mistakes in GSTR-3B values? +
Review values carefully before filing. Corrections may be handled through subsequent returns/adjustments as per GST provisions; maintain proper records and seek professional guidance if needed.
