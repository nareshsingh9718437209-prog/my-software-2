Invoice Creation Guide

1

Enter invoice number, date and other reference details in the top row of the form.

2

Upload your company logo in the dashed box (recommended size 160×80 px).

3

Fill in your business details under "Invoice From" — name, address, city, state.

4

Add your client's details under "Invoice For" using the same field layout.

5

Optionally click "+ Add Email / Phone / GST / PAN" to attach tax info to either party.

6

Add product rows with HSN code, GST rate, quantity and rate — amount is auto-calculated.

7

Choose Intra-state or Inter-state to apply the correct GST breakdown.

8

Add terms & conditions in the bottom field if required by your business.

9

Click "Download" to save the invoice or "Create New" to start over.

What is HSN Code?

HSN (Harmonised System of Nomenclature) is an internationally standardized system of names and numbers to classify traded products. GST invoices in India require HSN codes for goods.

HSN CodeProduct CategoryGST Rate
0101Live animals: horses, asses, mules0%
1006Rice0%
1701Cane or beet sugar5%
3004Pharmaceutical medicines12%
6109T-shirts, singlets and other vests5%
8517Telephone sets; smartphones18%
8703Motor cars and other motor vehicles28%

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the online invoice generator really free?+

Yes. You can create and download unlimited invoices at no cost. No login or registration is required.

Do I need to install any software?+

No. The generator runs entirely in your web browser. For advanced features like inventory and GST filing, you can use DotERP.

Is my data stored on your servers?+

No. All the details you type stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded — making it safe for sensitive business data.

Can I add GST and PAN to the invoice?+

Yes. Under both "Invoice From" and "Invoice For" panels click "+ Add GST" and "+ Add PAN" to include the tax identifiers.

Does it support intra-state and inter-state GST?+

Yes. Choose Intra-state (CGST + SGST) or Inter-state (IGST) using the radio buttons below the product table.
