Secure Data

Role-based access and backup options to keep your business data safe.

Access control

Control who can view or modify information in your system based on roles and responsibilities.

  • Role-based access: give users access only to the modules and actions they need
  • User accountability: maintain clearer responsibility for business operations
  • Reduced risk: limit unintended changes by restricting sensitive areas

Backup & data protection

  • Backup options: keep copies of important business data as per your setup and plan
  • Recovery readiness: help restore data in case of accidental loss or issues (as supported by your environment)
  • Safer operations: protect critical financial, inventory, and compliance records

Good practices

For best results, use strong credentials, review user permissions regularly, and maintain scheduled backups according to your business needs.
