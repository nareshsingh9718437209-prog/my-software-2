Add-on for Generating Financial Statements for Corporates and Non-Corporates

Powered by DotERP's simplicity and a smart Excel-based template to prepare ready-to-file statements from your books.

* Compatible with DotERP Release 7.1 or later and Microsoft Excel 2016 or later (demo flow on this site).

Generate financial statements for corporates and non-corporates with DotERP

The Financial Statements Add-on for DotERP helps you generate financial statements as per Schedule III Division I (for corporates) and for non-corporate entities, in line with applicable guidelines. The add-on works with an Excel-based template that auto-populates Trial Balance ledgers and closing balances from DotERP.

Once enabled, the add-on integrates directly into the Export menu (Alt+E) of Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss A/c, and Trial Balance. Configure the data type, period, and file path, then export â DotERP populates the Excel template with your data, ready for mapping, validation, and printing.

03. How to generate financial statements?

Getting Started

  • Open the Excel template saved in the DotERP installation folder.
  • Review the details in the Basic Information sheet.
  • Enter or verify company details, rounding-off method, and signatory/capital details, if required.

Mapping of Data

  • Review closing balances populated for current and previous financial years.
  • Check pre-mapped ledgers and update them, if required.
  • Add required details in the Input sheet, bifurcate balances, and provide disclosures to generate notes.
  • Identify and complete fields highlighted in yellow (e.g. unmapped ledgers or missing details).

Validate & Print Statements

  • Complete the remaining mappings and disclosures.
  • Review the financial statements for accuracy.
  • Print or export the financial statements in the required format.

How to Disable the Financial Statements Add-on?

  1. Enter the DotERP Serial Number in the field on the right.
  2. Click "Disable Add-on" to verify your serial number.
  3. Enter the OTP received on your registered e-mail ID.
  4. The bundled add-on is disabled for your DotERP license.
Once disabled, the Financial Statements option will no longer appear in the Export menu. You can re-enable the add-on at any time by entering your serial number above.

FAQ

Can I enable the financial statements add-on in DotERP? +
Yes. Enter your DotERP serial number on this page and click "Enable Add-on". The add-on can be enabled for your license after OTP verification (demo flow shown on website). Use a compatible DotERP release and Microsoft Excel 2016 or later.
How will I receive the OTP? +
The OTP is sent to the e-mail ID registered against your DotERP serial number. Ensure your registered e-mail is up to date before initiating enable or disable.
Do I need an active subscription/service plan to use the add-on? +
An active DotERP Software Service/subscription may be required to enable and use bundled add-ons, depending on your license and plan. Contact support for your account-specific eligibility.
How can I export and generate statements? +
After enabling, use Export (Alt+E) from Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss A/c, or Trial Balance, choose Financial Statements, configure period/path, export to the Excel template, complete mapping/disclosures, then validate and print.
Can I disable the add-on? +
Yes. Use the Disable section on this page with your serial number and OTP verification. You can re-enable it anytime.
What if I am using an older DotERP release? +
The add-on requires a compatible DotERP release (recommended latest) and Microsoft Excel 2016 or later. Please upgrade DotERP to enable and use this add-on.
